2. Description of TradeForge and Its Features

TradeForge is a web-based trading journal, statistical analytics workspace, and post-trade review tool. Key features currently include:

  • Trade Journal & Portfolio Telemetry: Tracking net P&L, profit factor, win rate, drawdown metrics, calendar performance views, symbol/direction breakdowns, and trade logs.
  • Statement Import & Export: Client-side parsing of supported CSV, HTML, and Excel (XLSX) trade history exports (such as Exness, Zuperior, MT4/MT5 statement exports), alongside PDF, CSV, and JSON ledger exports.
  • Playbooks, Notes & Local MT5 Workspace: Personal discipline checklists, trading notes, and a client-side MetaTrader 5 preview/importer workspace.
  • Optional Google Sign-In & Google Drive Sync: OAuth 2.0 cloud persistence under My Drive / TradeForge in your personal Google Drive.
  • Fourge AI Strategy Assistant: Quantitative insights running either locally via our offline rule-based engine or online via Google Gemini API integration.
