1. Information We Collect and Process

TradeFourge (operated by [OPERATOR_NAME]) is a personal trading journal, performance analytics dashboard, and AI strategy assistant. Unlike traditional cloud SaaS platforms, TradeFourge does not operate a central user trade database—your trading records live in your browser's storage and, when you connect Google Sign-In, in your own Google Drive.

Depending on which features you use, TradeFourge processes the following categories of information:

  • Google Account Profile Information: When you sign in with Google, our backend receives your Google subject identifier (sub), email address (email), display name (name), profile photo URL (picture), and email verification status (email_verified). Non-secret public profile fields are cached in browser storage so the UI can identify your active workspace and separate local caches between accounts.
  • Email/Password Form Inputs: Although the sign-in card contains email/password form fields behind a configurable backend adapter, no email/password backend is enabled in the default deployment. If submitted while unconfigured, passwords are validated only in memory for length rules and are never stored in browser storage.
  • Trading Records & Imports: Trade entries you add manually or import from broker CSV, HTML, or Excel (XLSX) statements, including ticket or position identifiers, instrument symbols, trade side (buy/sell), lot volume, open/close timestamps, entry/exit prices, stop-loss and take-profit prices, commissions, swaps, realized P&L, close reasons, and deletion tombstones (deletedUids).
  • Journal Entries, Playbooks & Preferences: Your written notes, trading discipline checklist rules, display currency (USD or INR), starting account capital, selected theme (classic, midnight, or light), sidebar collapse state, and local simulated MT5 session state (tradefourge_mt5_session).
  • AI Chat Prompts & Usage Counters: Your Fourge AI conversation messages (stored locally in tradefourge_bot_chat_history), selected Gemini model, AI mode preference (online or offline), any optional Gemini API key you manually enter into browser storage (fourge_gemini_api_key), and aggregate server token counters (inputTokens, outputTokens, usedTokens).
  • Technical Request Metadata: Standard HTTP request metadata (such as IP address, user-agent, request path, and timestamps) processed transiently by Vercel hosting infrastructure and serverless function logs when serving pages or executing /api/* routes.
Your Responsibility for Trading Data
You are solely responsible for the accuracy, legality, privacy hygiene, and independent backup of the broker statements, trade records, and journal notes you upload or store through TradeFourge. Do not paste brokerage passwords, banking PINs, tax IDs, or unrelated sensitive personal records into notes or AI prompts.

2. Google Sign-In and OAuth 2.0 Authentication

When you click Continue with Google or Sign up with Google, TradeFourge initiates a server-side OAuth 2.0 Authorization Code flow with PKCE (S256 code challenge) via /api/auth/google (and /api/drive/authorize for re-authorization).

  • A short-lived cryptographic CSRF state parameter and PKCE verifier are sealed in an HttpOnly cookie (tf_oauth_tx, expiring after 10 minutes) before redirecting you to Google's authorization screen.
  • On callback at /api/auth/callback, the server validates the returned state, exchanges the authorization code with Google's token endpoint using the server-held client secret and PKCE verifier, verifies the Google ID token via Google's tokeninfo endpoint (checking aud, sub, and email_verified), and fetches your basic profile.
  • OAuth access_token and refresh_token credentials stay strictly on the server inside encrypted HttpOnly session cookies (tf_sess) and are never sent to browser JavaScript or written into browser localStorage.

3. Google Drive Integration

When authenticated with Google, TradeFourge uses your personal Google Drive as your cloud storage location rather than storing your trading ledger in a shared TradeFourge server database.

All Drive requests are executed server-side through /api/drive/* endpoints using your sealed session cookie, organizing data exclusively under your personal folder hierarchy:

Google Drive Folder Path Purpose & Contents
My Drive / TradeFourge / User Data/ Primary synced JSON files for your trades ledger, deletion tombstones, journal notes, discipline checklist rules, display preferences (currency, starting capital), and local MT5 workspace state.
My Drive / TradeFourge / Backups/ Automatic safety snapshots created before cloud restores/merges and manual backups initiated by you from Settings.
My Drive / TradeFourge / Exports/ Exported report or ledger files written to your Drive when you trigger cloud export actions.
My Drive / TradeFourge / Database/ (Legacy Read Only) If you used an earlier version of TradeFourge that stored files inside Database/, TradeFourge checks that folder strictly for backwards-compatible migration reads.

When multiple devices sync to the same Google account, TradeFourge uses optimistic revision checks and three-way merging with deletion tombstones so additions, edits, and deletions synchronize consistently across sessions.

4. Google API Permissions and OAuth Scopes

TradeFourge follows the principle of least privilege and never requests broader Google permissions than the application requires. During Google Sign-In, TradeFourge requests only the following four OAuth scopes:

OAuth Scope Why TradeFourge Requests It
openid Verifies your identity via an OpenID Connect ID token bound to your unique Google account subject ID (sub).
email Displays your connected email address in your account profile menu and identifies the active account session.
profile Displays your name and Google profile avatar in the TradeFourge header, account dropdown, and Settings screen.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file Grants per-file access only to folders and JSON files created or opened by TradeFourge inside My Drive / TradeFourge.

TradeFourge does not request broad Google Drive scopes such as full-drive read/write (.../auth/drive) or read-only all-files access (.../auth/drive.readonly), and cannot view, list, or edit unrelated documents, photos, or spreadsheets stored elsewhere in your Google Drive. Because cloud journal sync is core to Google Sign-In, the backend rejects sign-in if the drive.file permission is declined and immediately revokes any under-scoped token.

Google API Services User Data Policy & Limited Use Disclosure
TradeFourge's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data accessed through Google Sign-In and drive.file is used solely to provide and maintain your user-facing trading journal, cloud synchronization, backup, and account interface features—never for advertising, data brokerage, or training third-party AI models on your Google Drive contents.

5. Fourge AI and Google Gemini API Integration

TradeFourge's Fourge AI assistant operates in two user-selectable modes:

  • Offline Mode (AI OFF): Uses the built-in local analytical engine (offline-strategy-engine.js) inside your browser. No prompts or trading metrics leave your device in Offline Mode.
  • Online Mode (AI ON): When you submit a question in Online Mode, your browser sends your prompt—together with a structured trading performance summary derived from your active dataset—to TradeFourge's server endpoint (/api/strategy-bot, up to 50,000 characters per payload, plus an optional previous_interaction_id for multi-turn continuity).

What Trading Context May Be Sent to Google Gemini in Online Mode

To answer data-driven performance questions, the Online Mode system prompt assembled on your device includes:

  • Aggregate metrics (total trade count, net P&L, gross profit/loss, win rate, profit factor, average win/loss, risk-to-reward ratio, drawdown, win/loss streaks, long/short breakdown, average hold times, and active display currency).
  • Per-symbol performance summary and weekday P&L totals.
  • Details of up to your top 5 best trades, top 5 worst trades, and 10 most recent executions (ticket number, symbol, buy/sell side, P&L, open timestamp, and hold duration).

The /api/strategy-bot route forwards this payload over HTTPS to Google's Generative Language / Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) using configured server API keys. Under fallback conditions (for example, opening the app locally via file:// or when the server proxy is unavailable or unconfigured), the browser may attempt a direct request to Google Gemini if a Gemini API key is configured. Once transmitted to Google Gemini, Google processes the prompt under Google's own API terms and privacy policies. You can toggle Online AI off at any time directly inside the Fourge AI view.

6. Local Browser Storage, Offline Caching, and Cloud Sync

TradeFourge stores operational state locally in your browser so charts, reports, and offline editing remain fast even without an internet connection:

  • Browser localStorage & sessionStorage: Signed-out mode stores journal data under standard tradefourge_* keys. When signed into Google, data is isolated under an account-specific hash prefix derived from your Google sub (tradefourge_acct_<hash>_*) so multiple Google accounts used in the same browser never mix trade histories. Public non-secret Google profile metadata and UI preferences (theme, currency, sidebar collapse state, AI chat history, token usage cache) are also kept locally. If you uncheck “Remember me” (or pass remember=0), the UI session state stays in sessionStorage (tradefourge_auth_session_tmp) rather than persistent localStorage.
  • First-Login Safe Migration: Signing into a Google account does not automatically overwrite or delete pre-existing signed-out local data; TradeFourge prompts you whether to migrate, merge, use cloud data, or keep signed-out storage separate.
  • PWA Service Worker Cache (sw.js): If registered by your browser, TradeFourge's service worker caches static frontend files (HTML, CSS, JS, icons, manifest) and third-party stylesheet/chart scripts for offline availability. It explicitly bypasses caching for all /api/* routes, accounts.google.com, googleapis.com, and googleusercontent.com traffic.

7. Data Security, Session Handling, and Access Controls

We describe only the technical safeguards verified directly in TradeFourge's source code:

  • Encrypted Server-Sealed Cookies: OAuth tokens and session payloads inside tf_sess and tf_oauth_tx cookies are deflate-compressed and authenticated-encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a 32-byte key derived via HMAC-SHA256 from the server's SESSION_SECRET environment variable. Cookies are marked HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and Secure over HTTPS connections.
  • No Token Exposure in Browser Storage: Google OAuth access_token, refresh_token, and GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET values are never included in client API responses or saved in localStorage/sessionStorage.
  • CSRF, Origin & Replay Protections: State-changing Drive sync and logout routes require POST requests with strict Same-Origin validation against request Origin/Referer headers. OAuth flows enforce CSRF state and PKCE verification, and signing out records the session identifier (jti) in a per-instance revocation registry to reject replay attempts within that runtime instance.
Transparent Security Scope & Non-Claims
  • Drive Storage Format: Journal files saved in My Drive / TradeFourge are stored as standard JSON protected by HTTPS in transit and your own Google Account security/permissions—they are not encrypted with a separate client-side zero-knowledge vault key.
  • No Claimed Certifications: TradeFourge has not undergone formal external security audits, penetration testing certifications, SOC 2, or ISO 27001 attestation. Overall protection relies on HTTPS/TLS, Google Account security (such as 2-Step Verification), server environment secrets, and the physical/OS security of your browser device.

9. Data Retention, Backup, Restoration, and Deletion

  • Local Browser Data: Trade records, notes, settings, and AI chat history persist in browser storage until you purge them via Settings (“Purge All Trades”), the sidebar (“Clear Data”), Fourge AI (“Reset Chat”), or clearing your browser's site data.
  • Google Drive Files & Backups: Files inside My Drive / TradeFourge / User Data/, Backups/, and Exports/ remain in your personal Google Drive under your control until you delete them in Google Drive or overwrite them from Settings. Automatic safety snapshots created prior to restores/merges stay available in Backups/ for manual restoration.
  • Session Cookies: OAuth transaction cookies (tf_oauth_tx) expire after 10 minutes. Session cookies (tf_sess) expire after 24 hours by default or up to 30 days when “Remember me” is selected, or immediately upon Sign Out.
  • Server Aggregate AI Telemetry: Server usage tracking (usage.json / temporary runtime file) retains cumulative prompt/output token counts across server Gemini API slots to monitor quota thresholds and does not store your prompt text or trade dataset.

10. How to Disconnect Google and Revoke Access

You may disconnect your Google account and revoke TradeFourge's permissions at any time using two straightforward steps:

  1. Sign Out inside TradeFourge: Click your profile avatar in the top-right header (or open Settings → Google Account & Google Drive Cloud Storage) and click Sign Out. TradeFourge calls /api/auth/logout, sends a revocation request to Google's OAuth /revoke endpoint for your active session token, marks the session revoked, clears HttpOnly session cookies, and switches your browser back to anonymous local mode without erasing existing files stored in your Google Drive.
  2. Revoke Access directly in Google Account Settings: Visit Google Account → Third-party apps & services, select TradeFourge, and click Remove all connections you have with TradeFourge. To remove cloud copies permanently, delete the TradeFourge folder in your Google Drive (and empty Google Drive Trash if desired).
